## 5. CORE FEATURES SECTION

### Section Label

`How Fydinn Works`

### Section Headline

> **Built around the things that actually matter.**

---

### Feature 01 — Interest-Based Matching

**Feature Truth:**
During onboarding, users select from 60+ interest categories spanning art, music, fitness, travel, gaming, food, film, tech, and more. The matching engine factors these shared interests into profile ranking and discovery.

**On-Page Headline:**

> Your interests are your introduction.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> Before you say a word, Fydinn already knows you love hiking at sunrise, are obsessed with Studio Ghibli, and can't live without good coffee. It finds people who feel the same way.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel **confident walking into a conversation** because the common ground is already there. The fear of "what do I even say?" disappears. Copy should evoke the feeling of meeting someone at a party who knows all the same niche things you know — that rare, electric "wait, you too?" moment.

**Visual Direction:**
A soft, animated grid of interest tags lighting up as they overlap between two profiles. Keep it playful and colourful.

---

### Feature 02 — Intention Setting

**Feature Truth:**
During onboarding, users declare one of three relationship intentions: Serious Relationship, Casual Dating, or Friendship. The app filters and surfaces profiles that share the same declared intent.

**On-Page Headline:**

> Know what you're walking into — every time.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> Nobody should waste months figuring out if you're on the same page. On Fydinn, everyone says what they're looking for upfront — a serious relationship, something casual, or a genuine friendship. You will only meet people who want what you want.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel **safe and respected** — like their time will not be wasted. Many users have been burned by ambiguity. This feature resolves the silent anxiety of "but what are they actually looking for?" Copy should feel like a sigh of relief, not a feature announcement.

**Visual Direction:**
Three clean cards — each with an icon, a label, and a short descriptor. Consider a soft animation where choosing one illuminates the path forward.

---

### Feature 03 — Video Introduction Profile

**Feature Truth:**
Users upload a short video during onboarding alongside their photos. This video is displayed on their profile card in the discovery feed.

**On-Page Headline:**

> See them. Hear them. Know them — before you ever say hello.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> Photos tell you what someone looks like. A video tells you how they laugh, what lights them up, and whether their energy matches yours. Fydinn profiles go beyond the surface.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel **less like they're gambling and more like they're making an informed choice**. The emotional hook is about reducing the gap between "profile impression" and "real person." Copy should evoke the feeling of watching someone's eyes light up when they talk about their passion — intimate, unguarded, real.

**Visual Direction:**
A phone mockup showing a profile card with a subtle video play button. The video background could show someone talking naturally, not posed.

---

### Feature 04 — Discovery Feed (Swipe/Scroll Interface)

**Feature Truth:**
The main discovery screen presents user profile cards vertically. Users can scroll through cards and initiate connections via the "Intent" system.

**On-Page Headline:**

> A feed that feels like possibility, not pressure.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> No frantic swiping. No split-second judgements. Fydinn gives you the space to actually look — to read a bio, watch a video, see what someone is about — and then decide if you want to connect.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel **in control and unhurried**. Most dating apps create anxiety through gamification and scarcity. This feature is the antidote. The copy should make the user feel like they are browsing a curated collection, not racing a clock.

**Visual Direction:**
An elegant phone mockup showing the discovery card stack — profile photo, name, age, distance, interest tags, and a chemistry score badge visible.

---

### Feature 05 — The Intents System

**Feature Truth:**
Instead of a mutual "swipe right" match, Fydinn uses "Intents" — a user sends an Intent to another profile. Both a "You Sent" and "Sent To You" inbox allows users to manage their connections deliberately.

**On-Page Headline:**

> Show genuine interest. No games, no guessing.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> On Fydinn, reaching out is an intentional act. Send an Intent to someone you're genuinely interested in. They'll see it, think it over, and respond when they're ready. It's connection, not competition.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel **empowered and dignified**. Sending the first message on conventional apps feels vulnerable and rejection-heavy. Intents reframe reaching out as an act of confidence, not desperation. Copy should feel assertive but warm — the emotional equivalent of leaving a thoughtful note versus hoping someone notices you across the room.

**Visual Direction:**
A split UI showing two tabs — "Sent" and "Received" — each with clean, card-based profile thumbnails. Subtle heart/spark icons to show states (pending, accepted, etc.).

---

### Feature 06 — Explore & Discovery Categories

**Feature Truth:**
The Explore screen organises discoverable profiles into five sections:

- **Ready to Talk** — Users open to receiving Intents
- **Nearby** — Users within proximity
- **Looking for the Same Thing** — Users sharing your declared intention
- **New Members** — Recently joined users
- **Popular Profiles** — Profiles with high engagement

**On-Page Headline:**

> Browse by what matters most to you right now.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> Looking for someone nearby? Someone who wants exactly what you want? Someone fresh to the platform? Fydinn's Explore section organises real people around the things that are most relevant to _you_, today.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel like they have **agency over how they search** — like walking into a party and being able to choose which room to go to. This is about personalised navigation without overwhelming the user. Copy should feel curated and smart, not like a grid of strangers.

---

### Feature 07 — Detailed Profile Depth (Chemistry Score, Lifestyle, Family Plans)

**Feature Truth:**
Profiles include height, lifestyle preferences (smoking, drinking), family plans, and a computed Chemistry Score based on shared attributes and interests.

**On-Page Headline:**

> Real compatibility lives in the details.

**On-Page Body Copy:**

> You should never have to ask "wait, do you want kids?" on a third date. Fydinn surfaces the things that matter early — lifestyle, values, future plans — so you can make real decisions from a place of real information.

**Feelings Translation:**

> Users should feel **emotionally protected** — like the app is doing the heavy lifting to avoid future heartbreak. The copy should resonate with anyone who has been surprised by a dealbreaker late in a connection. This is not about being rigid; it is about being wise.

**Visual Direction:**
A close-up of a profile detail card showing Chemistry Score badge, interest chips, and lifestyle icons (subtle, non-clinical icons for height, lifestyle, family plans).

### Section Label

`Get Started in Minutes`

### Section Headline

> **From download to first connection — here's what to expect.**

### Steps

| Step  | Icon Concept           | Headline                                   | Body                                                                                           |
| ----- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1** | Sparkle / profile card | Build a profile that reflects the real you | Add your photos, record a short video intro, and pick the interests that define your world.    |
| **2** | Target / goal flag     | Tell us what you're looking for            | Serious relationship, casual dating, or friendship — you set the tone from day one.            |

### Feelings Translation — Steps Section

> The user should feel that the **onboarding is intentional, not tedious**. Every step has a purpose that benefits _them_. Frame each step as an investment in a better outcome, not a form to fill out. The energy here should be anticipatory — like prepping for something exciting that is about to happen.

## 14. TONE OF VOICE GUIDE (For Copywriters & Designers)

| Attribute       | Do                                                        | Don't                                               |
| --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Warmth**      | Write like a trusted friend giving advice                 | Sound like a press release or tech startup          |
| **Honesty**     | Name the real problem without drama                       | Use fear or urgency as a selling tactic             |
| **Confidence**  | Be assertive about what Fydinn does differently           | Bash competitors or be defensive                    |
| **Specificity** | Reference real interests, real feelings, real scenarios   | Use vague superlatives ("amazing," "revolutionary") |
| **Inclusivity** | Welcome all genders, orientations, and intentions equally | Default to heteronormative or gendered language     |
| **Simplicity**  | Short sentences. Active voice. One idea at a time.        | Dense paragraphs, passive voice, jargon             |
