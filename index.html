Frequently Asked Questions

Which devices is the FirstDate app available on?

FirstDate is available as a free download on both iOS (iPhone, iPad) and Android via the App Store and Google Play.

Is identity verification mandatory?

Yes — every member completes a brief in-app verification step before they can interact, which is how we keep trust levels high.

Does the app work in my city?

FirstDate is live in major urban centers globally and expanding to new regions every month. Download the app to check coverage near you.

Is the app free?

Downloading and using the core features is completely free. A premium in-app tier unlocks extras like travel mode and unlimited likes.

How does the matching algorithm work?

Our AI analyzes your stated values, lifestyle preferences, and interaction patterns to surface compatible matches — not just looks and proximity.

How do you keep the platform safe?

Beyond mandatory ID verification, we use AI-powered content moderation, end-to-end encryption on messages, and a dedicated trust and safety team available around the clock.
