Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Short version: We collect what we need to match you with compatible people and keep the platform safe. We never sell your personal data to third parties. You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, information generated through your use of the app, and — where permitted — information from third-party verification services.

  • Profile data: Name, date of birth, photos, bio, preferences, and relationship intentions.
  • Identity verification data: Government-issued ID documents processed by our verification partner. We do not store raw document images after verification is complete.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged with other members, subject to end-to-end encryption.
  • Usage data: Log data, device identifiers, IP address, and interaction events (e.g. profile views, match actions) to improve the service.
  • Location data: Approximate location (city level) to surface nearby matches. We never share precise GPS coordinates with other members.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the FirstDate platform and matching algorithm.
  • Verify member identities and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Send you notifications, updates, and transactional communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations and resolve disputes.
  • Conduct internal analytics and safety research to improve our services.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited information only in these circumstances:

  • With other members: Your public profile (name, photos, bio) is visible to other verified members according to your privacy settings.
  • With service providers: Trusted processors who help us run the platform (cloud hosting, payment processing, identity verification) under strict data processing agreements.
  • For legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users and the public.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. When you delete your account, we permanently delete your profile data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or necessary to resolve outstanding disputes.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, port, or delete your personal data. You can exercise most of these rights directly in your account settings. For requests not covered in the app, contact us at privacy@firstdate.app. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

6. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including TLS encryption in transit, AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive data, and regular penetration testing. No system is 100% secure, but we take every reasonable precaution to protect your information. If you discover a security vulnerability, please disclose it responsibly to security@firstdate.app.

7. International Transfers

FirstDate operates globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your home country, including countries that may have different data protection laws. When we transfer data outside the EEA, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

8. Contact

Questions? Our privacy team is here to help. Contact us at privacy@firstdate.app or write to: FirstDate App Ltd., Privacy Team, 1 Tech Square, London, EC2A 4NE.
